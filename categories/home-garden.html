What buyers should check

Measure furniture and doorways before arranging collection. For appliances, confirm dimensions, power requirements, age, service history and accessories. Ask the seller to demonstrate that electrical items work before payment.

Tips for sellers

Include dimensions, material, brand or model, age, condition and defects. Add clear photos and state whether collection or delivery is available and whether delivery costs extra.

Plan collection and installation

Measure the item and the route it must travel through, including doors, stairs and lifts. Ask whether dismantling, delivery or installation is included and who is responsible for transport. For appliances and power tools, check operating condition, safety features, plugs, accessories and service history. Agree on collection time and payment only after confirming the item matches its description.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a useful furniture listing? Add dimensions, material, age, condition, defects, clear photos and whether delivery is available.

What should I test before buying an appliance? Where practical, see it powered on and check its main functions, cables, controls and included accessories.
