What buyers should check

Ask for the syllabus, schedule, teaching format, total fee, refund terms and materials required. Verify any institution that promises a formal qualification. Be cautious of guaranteed exam results or job offers.

Tips for advertisers

Include subject, learner level, class format, location, schedule, teacher experience and fee structure. For classes involving children, explain supervision arrangements and avoid publishing private student details.

Choose learning services with clear expectations

Compare the syllabus, learner level, class size, teaching format, schedule, location, materials and total cost. Ask how progress is assessed and whether trial sessions or sample lessons are available. Verify institutional accreditation independently if a course claims to award a recognised qualification. Avoid providers who guarantee exam results, visas or jobs without credible evidence.

Frequently asked questions

What should a tuition or course listing include? Include the subject, learner level, teacher or institution details, class format, schedule, location and full fee information.

How do I check a qualification claim? Contact the relevant awarding body or institution through independently verified contact details.
