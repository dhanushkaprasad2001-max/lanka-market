What buyers should check

Meet the animal and observe its health and living conditions. Ask about age, vaccination, veterinary history and care needs. Do not support illegal wildlife trade, protected species sales, cruelty or irresponsible breeding. Check applicable Sri Lankan requirements.

Tips for sellers

Advertisers should describe age, breed where known, health history, temperament and care requirements honestly. Use genuine photos and never conceal known conditions.

Put animal welfare first

Before arranging a transfer, learn about the animal's diet, housing, exercise, social needs and expected veterinary costs. Meet the animal where it is kept and observe its condition and behaviour. Ask for vaccination and treatment records where relevant. Confirm that the transfer is lawful and never buy or sell protected wildlife. A qualified veterinarian can advise on health checks and ongoing care.

Frequently asked questions

What information should an animal listing provide? Include the animal's age, species or breed where known, health and vaccination history, temperament, care needs and location.

Should I accept an animal without seeing it? Arrange a responsible in-person assessment and verify its welfare and legal status first.
