What buyers should check

When checking a vehicle, verify its registration book, chassis and engine numbers, service records and ownership documents. Inspect it in daylight, arrange an independent mechanic’s check and take a test drive where suitable. Check for accident damage, warning lights, tyre wear and signs of water damage.

Tips for sellers

Sellers should include make, model, year, mileage, engine capacity, transmission, fuel type, condition and known faults. Upload genuine photos from several angles and state the district and contact details clearly.

Compare vehicles carefully

Compare similar vehicles by model year, mileage, engine size, fuel type, transmission, ownership history and documented maintenance. A low asking price should not replace an independent inspection. Confirm that the person selling the vehicle is authorised to do so and complete ownership-transfer steps through the proper authorities.

Frequently asked questions

What details should a vehicle ad include? Make, model, year, mileage, engine capacity, transmission, fuel, condition, district, asking price and clear photographs help buyers assess the vehicle.

Should I pay a deposit before seeing a vehicle? Avoid sending money before verifying the seller, vehicle and documents. Inspect the vehicle and agree on safe payment and transfer arrangements.
