What buyers should check

Test phones for screen damage, cameras, speakers, charging, battery condition and account locks. For laptops, check the keyboard, ports, display, battery and charger. Ask for proof of purchase where available and avoid devices that appear stolen or locked.

Tips for sellers

Sellers should list brand, model, storage, memory, age, condition and included accessories. Photograph the actual item and disclose scratches, faults and missing parts.

Check condition and compatibility

Ask for the exact model number and compare specifications rather than relying only on a product name. Check whether the device is locked to an account or network, whether the serial number and accessories are present, and whether a warranty can be verified. Test the main functions before paying. For electrical appliances, confirm the voltage, plug type, dimensions and safe operating condition.

Frequently asked questions

What should a phone or laptop ad include? Include the exact model, storage, memory, age, battery condition, accessories, known faults and genuine photos of the actual device.

How can I reduce the risk of buying a faulty item? Arrange an in-person test, check important functions and avoid paying in advance to an unverified seller.
