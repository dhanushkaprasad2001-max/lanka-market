What buyers should check

Check the employer, job title, duties, workplace, working hours and salary. Verify the organisation independently. Be cautious if someone asks for a registration fee, bank credentials, passwords or one-time codes as a condition of recruitment.

Tips for sellers

Employers should describe the role, skills, experience, qualifications, location, employment type and application method. Do not request unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Evaluate job opportunities

Applicants should check the employer's identity, job location, duties, working hours, salary range, contract type and application process. Research the organisation using independently found contact details. Never pay a recruitment fee or share bank credentials, passwords or one-time verification codes to obtain an interview. Employers should provide accurate role requirements and avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive information during an initial application.

Frequently asked questions

What should a job advertisement include? Include the job title, employer, responsibilities, required skills, location, working hours, salary information where possible and a legitimate application method.

Is it safe to pay to apply for a job? Be very cautious. Requests for advance fees, OTPs, PINs or bank login details are major warning signs.
