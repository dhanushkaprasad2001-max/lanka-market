What buyers should check

Check whether a more specific category is available. Read the description, ask about condition and inspect goods where practical. Do not buy illegal, stolen, counterfeit or restricted items.

Tips for advertisers

Use a specific title and explain what is included, condition, dimensions or specifications, location and price. Add genuine photos and disclose limitations or defects.

Describe miscellaneous items precisely

Choose the closest available category where possible so people can find the advertisement. Include the item's purpose, dimensions, model or material, condition, included parts, known defects and collection location. Use recent photographs of the actual item. Do not advertise stolen, counterfeit, restricted or unlawful goods, and do not use vague titles that hide what is being offered.

Frequently asked questions

When should I use the Other category? Use it only when the item or opportunity does not fit one of the dedicated VIKMA categories.

What makes a miscellaneous listing useful? A specific title, accurate description, price, district, genuine photos and clear condition details help users decide whether to contact you.
