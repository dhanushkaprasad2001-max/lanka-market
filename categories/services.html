What buyers should check

Ask what is included in a quote, whether materials and transport cost extra, the expected completion time and what happens if extra work is needed. For regulated or safety-critical work, verify qualifications, licences and insurance independently.

Tips for advertisers

Describe the service, experience, areas covered, working hours and how customers can request a quote. Be clear about call-out fees, materials and limitations.

Agree on the scope before work starts

Describe the task clearly and request a written estimate that separates labour, materials, transport and any call-out fee. Agree on the expected start and completion dates, payment stages, cleanup and how changes will be approved. For electrical, structural, transport or other regulated work, independently verify qualifications, licences and insurance before hiring.

Frequently asked questions

What should a service ad explain? State the service offered, experience, area covered, availability, quote process and any minimum or call-out charges.

Should I pay the full amount upfront? Agree on reasonable payment milestones and get the scope and price in writing before work begins.
