Privacy Policy Sri Lanka

Read how VIKMA handles account details, advertisements, contact information, messages and data needed to operate the marketplace.

Information users provide

When you register or use VIKMA, the service may process account information such as your email address, profile name and authentication details. Advertisements may include a title, description, category, district, price, contact number and uploaded photographs.

Public advertisements

Approved advertisements are visible to visitors. Information included in an advertisement, including seller contact details and photos, may be seen by the public. Do not include passwords, bank PINs, identity numbers or other sensitive information in public listing fields.

Accounts and messages

VIKMA uses Supabase for application data and authentication. Account features include listing management, favourites and buyer-seller messages. Access is subject to service security controls. Keep your login credentials private.

Hosting, cookies and advertising

VIKMA is hosted using GitHub Pages and includes Google AdSense advertising code. These providers may process technical information or use cookies and similar technologies according to their own policies and your settings. Review the relevant provider privacy notices for details.

Privacy requests

Do not send passwords, OTPs or sensitive identity information through public listings or messages. For privacy questions or requests, contact VIKMA at Dhanushkaprasad2001@gmail.com. Never include passwords, one-time codes or bank PINs in your message.

Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, publish and manage advertisements, support buyer-seller communication, respond to reports and support requests, prevent misuse and maintain the service. Public listing details are displayed so visitors can contact advertisers. Private account and message information is not intended to be displayed as a public listing.

Third-party services and advertising choices

VIKMA relies on GitHub Pages for website hosting and Supabase for authentication and application data. Google AdSense may use cookies, device information and similar technologies to provide, measure and personalise advertising where permitted by law and your settings. Google and other providers process information under their own privacy terms. You can review or change Google advertising personalisation choices through Google Ads Settings and your browser controls. If VIKMA serves visitors in regions where consent is required for advertising cookies, the site operator must configure an appropriate consent message through the relevant Google publisher settings before serving personalised ads.

Data security and retention

VIKMA applies access controls intended to limit access to account, listing and message data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained while an account, listing, report or support matter remains active, or for as long as reasonably necessary for safety, legal and operational purposes. For a privacy or account-data request, contact the site operator using the email address below. Do not include passwords, OTPs or bank PINs.
