About VIKMA | Sri Lankan Classifieds Marketplace

Learn about VIKMA, a Sri Lankan classifieds marketplace for discovering local goods, services and opportunities.

Our purpose

VIKMA is a Sri Lankan classifieds marketplace designed to make it easier to discover local advertisements across the country. Categories include vehicles, property, electronics, jobs, animals, home and garden, fashion, services and education.

People can explore approved advertisements, contact advertisers and submit their own listings for review. VIKMA is designed to work on mobile phones and desktop devices.

Review and free listings

New advertisements are submitted for administrator review before appearing publicly. Standard advertisements are free to submit. Users can report suspicious or misleading advertisements for review.

Independent checks matter

VIKMA is a platform for discovering advertisements, not a party to transactions. Buyers and sellers should independently verify people, goods, services, ownership documents and payment arrangements before completing a deal.
