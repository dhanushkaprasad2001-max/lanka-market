How VIKMA Works | Post Free Ads and Browse Sri Lanka

Learn how to browse categories, search by district, register, post a free advertisement and contact sellers on VIKMA.

1. Browse and search

Choose a category or use the search box to find an item, service or opportunity. Narrow results by district and compare the available details. Only approved advertisements are displayed publicly.

2. Contact the advertiser

Open an advertisement to read its description and review photos. Use the contact options supplied by the advertiser to ask questions and arrange an inspection. Verify important details independently before paying.

3. Register and post for free

Create an account or use Google sign-in. Complete the ad form with a clear title, category, district, price, contact number, description and genuine photos. Standard listings are free.

4. Wait for review

New ads remain pending until reviewed. Approved ads appear in public listings. You can manage your own ads from your account. Do not submit illegal, misleading, counterfeit or prohibited content.

5. Trade safely

Meet safely, inspect items before paying and keep a record of agreements. Never share passwords, bank PINs or one-time verification codes. Read the safety guide.
